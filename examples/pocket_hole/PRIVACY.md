# Pocket Hole privacy information

Pocket Hole saves the current unlocked level and the haptics preference on the device using the operating system's preferences storage. The game does not include advertising, analytics, account creation, tracking SDKs, or a backend service. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photo access.

Removing app data or uninstalling the app removes local preferences, subject to the operating system's backup/restore behavior.

Before publishing, the publisher must add their legal identity, support contact, effective date, and a hosted policy URL, and verify that this description matches the exact binary they distribute and any SDKs they add.
