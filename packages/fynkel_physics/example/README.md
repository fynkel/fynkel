# fynkel_physics example

Not a lesson: a benchmark. A pile of cubes falls, tumbles and settles,
dropped again every 8 seconds, on the raw world of stage 5 step 1, to
measure the physics step (`tool/measured.dart`, and the
`--dart-define`s at the top of `lib/main.dart`).

To learn the physics, read the guide's §14
([docs/writing-a-game.md](../../../docs/writing-a-game.md)) and the
[bowling](../../../examples/bowling) example: rigidbodies, a mesh
collider and gestures in a small game.
